- This is a fork of [https://github.com/philffm/ai-summary-helper](https://github.com/philffm/ai-summary-helper), I want to transform the original project into a tool for idea extraction on the web. I don't really requires summarizing. In my use case, the webpage that requires analyzing often has multiple blocks of text / threads of discussion that focus on unrelated topics. And inside a single block of text, there could be multiple key ideas being presented. I want to AI be able to identify those key ideas or arguments as they appear, regardless of whether there are too many, then highlight the key sentences where the ideas are presented. I don't want the AI to focus on or highlight the logical process or background context or supporting material that leads to a specific argument. Aside from the highlights, in the main response, I want to AI to point out succinctly the content in the page that are highly valuable (novel or insightful) but I could miss if only looking at highlights.

The default highlight prompt is:

> Highlight the exact sentences that directly state key ideas or arguments.
>
> The page may contain multiple unrelated topics or threads. Treat each independently, and include as many distinct ideas as needed.
>
> Prefer claims, conclusions, interpretations, recommendations, predictions, or distinctions. Skip background, reasoning steps, evidence, examples, anecdotes, and repetition unless they express an independent idea.
>
> When several sentences make the same point, highlight the clearest one.



The default summary prompt is:

> Briefly surface valuable content that the highlights may miss.
>
> Focus on subtle qualifications, implications, disagreements, cross-page patterns, surprising connections, or important context.
>
> Do not repeat highlighted ideas or give a general summary. Keep it concise and information-dense.
